# Harborline Outfitters — Weekly Business Review notes, August and September 2026
Owner: Chief Operating Officer's office · Harborline Outfitters is a fictional retailer used for demonstration.

## WBR 10 August 2026
- West footwear in-stock rate fell below 80% at Reno DC for Boots and Running Shoes. Cause: Pacific Stride allocation cut (see HLO-INV-008 section 5). Lost sales units in West footwear roughly doubled week over week. Action: Category planner (Footwear) to size a DC-to-DC transfer. Owner: M. Alvarez.
- Southeast on-time delivery for USPS Standard dropped after the USPS Atlanta sort facility consolidation. Action: Transportation to monitor for two weeks under HLO-OPS-022 section 5. Owner: J. Chen.

## WBR 24 August 2026
- Southeast USPS on-time remained below 85% for the second week. Carrier incident TR-2026-031 opened on 18 August. UPS capacity at Atlanta DC is the constraint for re-routing.
- Back-to-school promotion (from 1 August) lifted Sneakers and Bags & Backpacks everywhere except West footwear, where stock was not available.
- Free shipping % continues to run about 9 points below last year. This is the intended effect of the March threshold change (HLO-POL-014); AOV is up. No action.

## WBR 14 September 2026
- Southwest plan attainment year to date is about 88%, the lowest of all regions. Causes: only one of three new stores opened, and softer online demand in TX and AZ. FP&A to present recovery options before the 15 October re-forecast. Owner: R. Okafor.
- Re-routing Southeast light parcels from USPS to UPS approved; starts 5 October 2026.
- West footwear transfer of 15,000 pairs approved on 2 September; first truck arrived at Reno DC on 21 September.

## WBR 28 September 2026
- West footwear in-stock improving but still below target; full recovery expected after the 20 October replenishment cycle.
- Labor Day sale performance in line with last year.
- Decision needed: whether to extend the Southeast UPS re-routing to Florida marketplace orders.
