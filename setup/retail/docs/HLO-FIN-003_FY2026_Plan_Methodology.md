# Harborline Outfitters — FY2026 Sales Plan Methodology
Document HLO-FIN-003 · Approved by the Executive Committee on 9 December 2025 · Owner: FP&A
Harborline Outfitters is a fictional retailer used for demonstration.

## 1. How the plan is built
The FY2026 plan is set monthly by region and category. Each month's plan equals the same month's 2025 actual net sales multiplied by the region's growth target, then phased to days using expected order volume by day of week and promotion calendar.

## 2. Growth targets by region (net sales, vs 2025)
- Northeast: +6%
- Southeast: +7%
- Midwest: +5%
- Southwest: +10% (includes three new stores planned in Austin, San Antonio and Phoenix)
- West: +8%

## 3. Southwest risk noted at approval
FP&A flagged the Southwest target as aggressive. The three new stores were planned to open in Q1 2026, but by the end of Q2 only the Austin store had opened, and Southwest online demand has been softer than forecast since January 2026. The plan was not re-baselined; variance is explained monthly in the WBR.

## 4. Plan metrics
Plan net sales and Plan units are available per day, region and category. Plan attainment = actual net sales divided by plan net sales. Net sales vs plan = actual net sales minus plan net sales.

## 5. Re-forecast
A Q4 re-forecast will be issued on 15 October 2026. Regions below 95% plan attainment year to date must submit recovery actions before then.
