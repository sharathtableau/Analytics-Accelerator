# Harborline Outfitters — Replenishment and In-Stock Standard
Document HLO-INV-008 · Version 2.4 · Effective 1 February 2026 · Owner: VP Supply Chain · Status: Approved
Harborline Outfitters is a fictional retailer used for demonstration.

## 1. In-stock target
Each DC must keep 95% of active SKUs in stock at end of day (In-stock rate). A SKU-DC-day with zero units on hand counts as a stockout day. The Stockout rate is stockout SKU-days divided by all active SKU-days.

## 2. Weeks-of-supply targets by category
- Apparel: 6 to 9 weeks of supply.
- Footwear: 7 to 10 weeks of supply (longer supplier lead times, size curves).
- Accessories: 5 to 8 weeks of supply.
Weeks of supply = units on hand divided by average weekly units sold over the last 4 weeks.

## 3. Replenishment cycle
Replenishment orders are placed every 14 days per SKU per DC and bring stock up to the target cover. A replenishment that brings less than 3 weeks of cover must be flagged to the category planner the same day.

## 4. Lost sales
When a DC has no stock, online demand for that SKU in the region cannot be fulfilled and is recorded as lost units. Lost sales (units) is demand that could not be served because of a stockout. Lost sales are not included in net sales.

## 5. Supplier exception — West footwear, 2026
Pacific Stride Footwear (fictional) supplies about 40% of Harborline footwear units. Port congestion at Oakland and a labor dispute at the supplier's consolidator reduced its shipments to the Reno DC from late July 2026. Allocation to Reno was cut to roughly 20% to 35% of normal replenishment for footwear from the cycle starting 25 July 2026. Supply Chain expects normal allocation to resume from the cycle starting 20 October 2026. Boots, Sneakers and Running Shoes in the West region are most affected. Mitigation approved 2 September 2026: transfer up to 15,000 pairs from Columbus DC and Dallas DC to Reno DC; first transfer arrived 21 September 2026.

## 6. Escalation
If a category's in-stock rate at a DC is below 85% for two consecutive weeks, the category planner must present a recovery plan in the Weekly Business Review.
